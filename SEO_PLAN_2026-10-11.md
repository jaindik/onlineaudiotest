## Measurement cadence and stop rules

- **Daily for 7 days:** record complete-day clicks and impressions, deploy incidents, and index/serving errors. Use a rolling 7-day view only as an alert, not as a causal conclusion.
- **Weekly:** compare the latest complete 28 days with the previous 28 days by page/query, then segment the material losses by country and device. Use the same property, search type, and filters each time.
- **After snippet edits:** evaluate page + query CTR with position and impressions side by side. Avoid declaring a win from very small samples or a position change.
- **After technical fixes:** verify status, canonical, robots, rendered content, and sitemap immediately; inspect GSC indexing/performance over subsequent days.
- **Stop and investigate:** if a release coincides with broad deindexing, a sudden status/redirect failure, or a sustained loss in both GSC clicks and GA4 organic sessions. Roll back only when the release is credibly tied to the regression and rollback restores the expected behavior.
